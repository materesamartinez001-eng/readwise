I-RISE READ v2.1 — revised upload package
Upload index.html, manifest.json, and sw.js to the ROOT of your existing GitHub Pages repository. Replace the old files and commit the changes.

Revisions: “Read the Passage Aloud” recording control; removed broad decoding-notes field; vocabulary word bank shows words only, not definitions; vocabulary and comprehension checks each contain five questions; fourth section renamed “Students' Progress”; saved records include learner code, vocabulary score, comprehension score, and teacher-verified substitutions, omissions, and repetitions.

The included passages/questions are sample materials and should be checked against school-approved I-RISE 5.0 materials before formal use. Speech recognition varies by browser and may require internet; audio may be processed by the browser/provider. Transcript comparison cannot diagnose pronunciation or confirm miscues; teacher verification is required. Records stay in this browser/device only. Use learner codes and secure exported records. This is a formative aid, not a replacement for Phil-IRI or other approved assessment.
