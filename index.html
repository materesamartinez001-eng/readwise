QUESTION WORDS

Who – Used to ask about a person.

What – Used to ask about a thing.

Where – Used to ask about a place.

When – Used to ask about time, occasion, or moment.

Why – Used to ask about a reason.

Which – Used to ask about a choice.

Whose – Used to ask about possession.

How – Used to ask about a method or way.

THE ANTS

The ants saw a crumb on the ground.
They ran in a line to get it.
Each ant took a bite and went back home.
They worked hard and did not stop.
Soon, the crumb was gone!

Questions

  1. What did the ants see on the ground?
  2. How did they get the crumb?
  3. Why was the crumb gone?
  4. Are they able to get the crumb?
  5. Where do they put the crumb?

TIME TO SLEEP

The sun is down.
Kiko hops into bed.
Mom gives him a hug.
Dad kisses him.
He shuts his eyes.
Now, he sleeps well.

Questions

  1. When does the story happen?
  2. Where does Kiko go?
  3. Who gives Kiko a hug?
  4. Who kisses Kiko?
  5. What does Kiko do last?
